## High-Risk Mechanisms

### M-01 Message-create side-effect pipeline
**Anchor**: app/models/message.rb:11-12, app/models/room.rb:48-85, app/controllers/messages_controller.rb:20-28,79-85, app/models/webhook.rb:61-74.
**Purpose**: unread marking, push and bot webhooks for every new message (observed in the message, controller and system unread tests).
**Guarantees**: the message and its rich text commit together. Unread marking and the push enqueue are **after-commit** and non-atomic. The job store is volatile Redis. The webhook trigger lives in the controller, not the model, so webhook **replies never trigger webhooks**. That is the de facto loop prevention, and the port must keep it explicit.
**Failure history**: no fix commits on this path. Latent failure modes: crash between commit and `receive`; Redis down → `perform_later` raises after the message is saved, so the request returns 500 for a saved message (KB note on Campfire).
**Candidate**: REPLACE → a single `Chat.create_message/3` with the unread update, FTS row and Oban inserts in one `Repo.transact` on the writer, then PubSub. **Why not MAP**: copying the callback would keep the after-commit gap that Oban-in-transaction removes. Oban also moves delivery from at-most-once to at-least-once, which changes M-20 and M-21 semantics (see Q3).

### M-28 Presence stored in the DB drives unread and push suppression
**Anchor**: app/models/membership/connectable.rb, app/channels/presence_channel.rb, app/javascript/controllers/presence_controller.js (50 s refresh, 5 s visibility delay), config/puma.rb:50.
**Purpose**: business rule "you're looking at it, so it's not unread and you get no push" (observed in push_test and membership_test). The DB storage is a platform workaround (no shared memory across Puma workers). The TTL and the boot reset are reliability patches for missed unsubscribes.
**Guarantees**: eventual (60 s TTL). The counter is read-modify-write and lossy across tabs. Hidden tabs count as absent after 5 s.
**Candidate**: REPLACE → `Phoenix.Presence` tracked by the room LiveView, with track and untrack following visibility via a hook. Unread and push recipient queries exclude the user ids present in the room at commit. **Why not MAP**: DB writes every 50 s per tab compete for the single SQLite writer, and process-linked presence removes the TTL, the refresh and the boot reset. **Risk**: Presence is per node until clustered (fine for single-node ONCE). If the push job runs later, decide whether presence is sampled at commit (into job args) or at run time (Q4).

### M-07 / M-29 / M-27 Socket authorization and revocation
**Anchor**: app/models/user.rb:48-63, app/models/membership.rb:7, app/channels/room_messages_channel.rb, app/channels/concerns/room_streams_are_authorized.rb, app/controllers/concerns/authentication.rb:70-81.
**Purpose**: revoking a membership or session must stop delivery. The stock Turbo channel only checked a signature with no expiry (ee37809), and Cable never re-authorizes a live connection (9dd19d0).
**Guarantees**: authorization at subscribe, and revocation by forced reconnect. Sign-out disconnects best-effort, after the session row and cookie are gone. Deactivate and ban disconnect *inside* the transaction.
**Failure history**: ee37809 (harvested stream names kept working after revocation), 3b509f5 (global unread stream leaked activity timing), 5c5c82b (DM deletion and promotion through the wrong controller), 9dd19d0 (signed-out sockets stayed live).
**Candidate**: REPLACE → authorize in LiveView `mount`/`on_mount` (runs again on every reconnect) and re-check membership before rendering room events. Broadcast `:membership_revoked` and `"disconnect"` to `user:<id>` after commit. **Why not MAP**: stream-name signing and channel-class subscription are Turbo/Cable artifacts. Port the 8 room_messages_channel tests as LiveView authorization tests.

### M-36 / M-38 / M-37 / M-44 Capabilities signed with `SECRET_KEY_BASE`
**Anchor**: authentication.rb:93-99, session_lookup.rb, initializers/session_store.rb, lib/rails_ext/action_text_attachables.rb, user/avatar.rb, user/transferable.rb, blob URLs in messages/_actions.html.erb.
**Purpose**: login persistence (documented: changing the key "will invalidate sessions"), mention attachments in stored bodies, avatar and transfer capabilities, blob download links.
**Guarantees**: session cookies are permanent (20 y) and httponly. Mention sgids are already tolerated **unverified** (User only), so the stored-data dependency on the key is weaker than it looks.
**Failure history**: bebe518/77bcad6 (Rails 7 Marshal-format sgids crashed mentions after the Rails 8 upgrade).
**Candidate**: SHIM a one-way session-cookie reader and the sgid gid extractor; REPLACE the rest with `Phoenix.Token`. Whether to keep sessions across cutover is UNDECIDED (Q1).

### M-19 / M-20 / M-18 Bot API and webhooks (charter must-keep)
**Anchor**: config/routes.rb (`scope path: ":bot_key"`), app/controllers/messages/by_bots_controller.rb, messages/boosts/by_bots_controller.rb, app/models/webhook.rb, app/models/user/bot.rb.
**Purpose**: external bot integration (documented in SECURITY.md; heavily tested; hotspot with 4 fix commits).
**Guarantees**: auth by path key on every request; 404 for non-member rooms; webhook at-most-once with a 7 s timeout that posts a failure message; replies posted as the bot; no SSRF guard (intentional).

**Candidate**: MAP the wire byte-for-byte; REPLACE the job with Oban. **Open**: the retry policy (Q3), because a retried webhook can post a duplicate bot reply.

### M-25 / M-22 Volatile job and push infrastructure
**Anchor**: config/redis.conf (`appendonly no`, `save ""`), Procfile, config/environments/production.rb:96, app/jobs/application_job.rb, lib/web_push/pool.rb, config/initializers/web_push.rb.
**Purpose**: off-request work. **Guarantees provided today**: none durable, no retries, overflow silently dropped, pending pushes lost on deploy.
**Candidate**: REPLACE → Oban Lite in the same SQLite file. This **upgrades** durability and must be paired with idempotency decisions for push (duplicate notifications) and webhooks (duplicate replies). Under Iron Law 7 any *downgrade* must be recorded; here the change is an upgrade, but at-least-once is still a semantic change for consumers (Q3).

### M-34 Fragment caching with per-request content
**Anchor**: app/views/messages/_message.html.erb:3-4, messages/_actions.html.erb (boost `form_with`, `url` with host), users/sidebars/rooms/_direct.html.erb, *.json.jbuilder `json.cache!`.

**Hazard**: cached fragments carry the first renderer's CSRF token, request host and host-dependent embed filtering (inferred). They also go stale on creator name and avatar changes.
**Candidate**: DELETE. **Why not MAP**: porting the keys ports the leak. Cache only per-message filtered body HTML, and only if measured.

### M-17 IP ban (enqueue inside the transaction)
**Anchor**: app/models/user/bannable.rb:4-41, app/controllers/concerns/block_banned_requests.rb.
**Purpose**: documented in 30fe6ab. **Guarantees**: the enqueue happens before commit, Redis is volatile and there are no retries, so content removal is best-effort.
**Candidate**: REPLACE → Oban job in the same transaction with idempotent deletion. `remote_ip` must resolve identically behind Thruster or a proxy (otherwise bans hit the proxy IP).

## Implicit Framework Behavior (the port must re-provide)

1. **Atomic Action Text save**: `has_rich_text` autosaves `action_text_rich_texts` in the message transaction; an Ecto insert must write both in one `Repo.transact`.
2. **`insert_all` skips duplicates** on SQLite (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`); `Repo.insert_all` raises unless `on_conflict: :nothing` (M-06, M-11).
3. **`touch: true` cascades** boost → message → room inside the transaction (M-03).
4. **after_commit order = definition order** (`load_defaults 8.2`), so FTS indexing runs before unread and push (M-01, M-02).
5. **`has_one_attached` defaults**: `purge_later` on destroy and `analyze_later` after attach are implicit jobs (M-14, M-25).
6. **`PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON`** (Rails SQLite adapter): deletes must remove children first (M-14).
7. **`BEGIN IMMEDIATE` + 5 s busy timeout** (M-54).
8. **Signed cookie metadata and purpose** (`load_defaults` ≥ 6.0 cookies with metadata; JSON serializer) (M-36).
9. **`rate_limit` store** = `Rails.cache` (Redis) (M-47).
10. **`fresh_when`/`stale?`** emit ETag + Last-Modified and answer 304 (M-35).
11. **CSRF**: `protect_from_forgery with: :exception`, skipped for bot-key auth and PWA (M-18, M-65).
12. **`filter_parameters`** never covers path segments (M-53).
13. **Cable handshake-only auth** (M-07) and **Turbo signed stream names** (M-29). These go away with LiveView, but the authorization they lacked must be explicit.
14. **`request.remote_ip`** trusted-proxy rules (M-17, M-47).
15. **Strong params with empty strings**: Rails stores `""`, Ecto `cast` turns it into `nil` (e.g. `bio`, `name`); check NOT NULL columns.

## Questions

1. **Cutover compatibility (blocks M-36, M-37, M-44)**: must existing browser sessions, avatar and transfer URLs and old signed blob URLs keep working after cutover, or is a forced re-login with regenerated links acceptable? Settled by ADR-000 (cutover mode).
2. **Mention write format (M-38)**: after the port, are new mentions stored as Rails-format sgids (allows Rails rollback) or in a new attribute format? Settled by whether a rollback window is required.
3. **Delivery semantics under Oban (M-20, M-21, M-17)**: Oban gives at-least-once. Should webhooks retry (risking duplicate bot replies) or stay at-most-once (`max_attempts: 1`)? Should push dedupe per (message, subscription)? Settled by product or bot-owner input; the legacy behavior is at-most-once with no retries.
4. **Presence sampling time (M-28)**: compute unread and push recipients from presence at commit (stored in job args) or at job run? Legacy samples unread at commit and push at job run.
5. **Edge/TLS ownership (M-56)**: keep Thruster or move TLS and ACME into the release? Settled by the deployment ADR and ONCE requirements.
6. **SSRF policy ownership (M-43)**: who maintains the Elixir port of surfguard's ranges and tracks upstream changes?
7. **`X-Version`/`X-Rev` consumers (M-51)**: does ONCE or any monitoring read them?
8. **CSP (M-63)**: d3f22d7 refers to an "existing Content-Security-Policy", but the initializer is commented out, so `csp_meta_tag` emits nothing. Is a CSP expected (accepted gap or regression)?
9. **Sub-path deployments (M-69)**: is serving under `SCRIPT_NAME` a supported configuration?
10. **Direct-room uniqueness (M-12)**: add a member-set hash column (schema change) or rely on the serialized writer transaction?
